About MeetingMate AI

MeetingMate AI is a simple, explainable AI-powered meeting management application that helps teams track meeting duration and time-based attendance while automatically extracting action items, responsible persons, deadlines and confidence scores from meeting transcripts.

🤖

Key Features

⏱
Meeting Timer

Live countdown and elapsed time tracking with alerts.

👥
Time-Based Attendance

Calculates attendance percentage using strict minimum time thresholds.

🧠
AI Action-Item Extraction

Extracts tasks, owners, and deadlines with explainable confidence.

📊
Meeting History

Stores completed meetings and exports actionable reports to CSV.

Technologies Used

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript (ES6) Python (Flask) NumPy Python NLP Libraries

🎓 College Viva & Project Explanation Highlights

  • Attendance Logic: Attendance is marked Present strictly when Attended Time > Minimum Attendance Time (e.g. 25 min > 20 min is Present, 20 min is Absent).
  • Attendance Percentage: Calculated as (Attended Time / Total Meeting Duration) × 100.
  • AI/NLP Extraction: Uses Regex tokenization and keyword mapping to extract action verbs, speaker prefixes (Owners), and temporal entities (Deadlines).
  • NumPy Scoring: Computes weighted dot product across feature matrices for confidence scores.
